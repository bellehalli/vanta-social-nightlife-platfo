FAQs

Questions, Answered

Can I get in free before a certain time?

Some events may release guest-list or early-entry offers. The specific cutoff should appear on the event detail or guest-list confirmation.

Does joining the guest list guarantee entry?

No. Entry would still depend on capacity, arrival cutoff, valid ID and venue policies.

Can I celebrate a birthday without booking a table?

Yes. The guest-list form includes a birthday flag. A table is optional, though larger groups may benefit from a reserved section.

How do table minimums work?

The stated minimum represents the required food/beverage or bottle-service spend for the section. Deposits are shown separately in the VIP flow.

Can I wear sneakers?

A real venue would define its exact policy. This concept uses an upscale-and-intentional standard rather than a blanket footwear rule.

Where do I find tonight’s exact event details?

Use the Events page as the source of truth for event time, music, ticket tier and entry details.
